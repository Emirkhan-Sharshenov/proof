Questions

Does my client need an account?

No. They open a normal link in the browser. Nothing to install or sign up for.

What exactly do I see?

Which of your links was opened, when, which case studies were viewed and for how long. Each link is tied to the client you made it for.

What does “verified” mean?

The testimonial was written by your client through a private link you sent them. You can hide it, but you can’t change a word.

Can I leave and take my portfolio with me?

Yes. Download everything as a .zip — Markdown, images, CSV and JSON — at any time, on any plan.
