Refund Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Folioseen Pro is a subscription, billed monthly or yearly. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our reseller and merchant of record, so your receipt and card statement show Dodo Payments.

14-day money-back guarantee

If Pro isn’t right for you, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it in full. No questions asked.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel anytime in Settings → Plan → Manage subscription.
  • After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account moves to the Free plan. Your case studies, links and analytics stay; Pro-only images and testimonials stop showing until you upgrade again.
  • We don’t refund the unused part of a monthly or yearly period after the first 14 days, except where the law requires it.

Failed or duplicate charges

If you were charged twice or charged after cancelling, email us and we’ll refund the extra charge.

How to ask for a refund

Email sgoo0931@gmail.com from the address you used to pay, or reply to your receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
