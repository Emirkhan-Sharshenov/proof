Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Folioseen (“we”, “us”) helps freelancers share their work with clients through personal links and see how those links are read. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

If you have a Folioseen account

  • Your email address and, if you sign in with Google or GitHub, the basic profile those services share (name, email, profile picture). We never see your Google or GitHub password.
  • What you add: case studies, client names, notes and the links you create.
  • If you connect Telegram: your Telegram chat ID, so we can send you alerts.

If you open a link someone sent you

  • That you opened the page, when, and for how long each case study was open while the tab was visible.
  • Your browser’s user agent (to show “desktop” or “mobile”), the referring page if your browser sends it, and a random identifier stored in your browser so repeat visits within a short time count as one.
  • We do not collect your name, email or IP-based location, and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

This information is shown only to the freelancer who sent you the link.

If you join the waitlist — your email address and the page you came from.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, store your work, show link analytics and send the alerts you turned on.
  • To email waitlist members about early access. You can ask us to remove you at any time.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — database and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivering the website.
  • Telegram — delivering alerts, only if you connect it.
  • Google and GitHub — sign-in, only if you choose them.

These providers may process data in other countries under their own security and privacy commitments.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. Deleting a link deletes its visit history. Waitlist emails are kept until launch or until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete your case studies, links and visit history in the app at any time.
  • Disconnect Telegram in Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account by emailing sgoo0931@gmail.com.

Children

Folioseen is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let account holders know.
